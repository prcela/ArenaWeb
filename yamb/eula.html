10.5. Game fee: 4% in diamonds

The standard game fee described below is 4%, calculated in diamonds and deducted from the contributions used to fund rewards. It is an in-game fee, not a government tax or an additional charge to your payment card. A fee is included in the contributed diamonds, rather than added on top of the stated contribution. The rate, calculation and exceptions must be available before participation; a later fee change must not be applied retroactively to an entry already accepted.

  • Turn-based multiplayer (MP): when an entry contribution has been collected, 4% of each player's contribution is deducted, rounded down to a whole diamond for each player. The remaining contributions form the winner's reward. For two players contributing 1,000 diamonds each, the total fee is 80 diamonds and the winner receives 1,920 diamonds, including the remainder of their own contribution.
  • Ordinary One vs All (OVA): where there is more than one participating player, the reward pool is 96% of the collected entry contributions, rounded down to a whole diamond. With two or three players the first-ranked player receives that pool. With four or more players, it is divided 60%, 30% and 10% among the first three, with each award rounded down; small rounding remainders are not distributed. No game fee is deducted from the returned contribution in an ordinary OVA round with only one player.
  • Daily challenges with an entry contribution: the reward pool is 96% of the collected entry contributions, rounded down to a whole diamond. It is distributed among up to three eligible winners in proportions of 6:3:1, adjusted to the number of eligible winners. The last eligible winner receives any rounding remainder.

The standard 4% game fee does not apply to a game with no diamond contribution. Daily-reward rounds, donation rewards, free or beat-the-bot daily challenges and league entry arrangements have their own reward or entry rules; the calculations above must not be assumed to apply to those separate arrangements. Any separate entry cost must be disclosed before entry.

10.6. Separate gifting fee

When gifting is available, the standard fee-free daily sending limit is 2,500,000 diamonds per sending device, counted using the server's calendar day. A gift which takes the cumulative amount over that limit, and each further gift that day, requires the option allowing gifts above the limit. Such a gift has a separate 4% fee on its entire amount, not just the portion above the limit. The fee is rounded down to a whole diamond and deducted from what the recipient receives; the sender pays the stated gift amount. Without that option, the above-limit gift is rejected. 11. Connection interruptions, time limits and match review

11.1. Reconnecting and completing a turn. In turn-based multiplayer matches, a disconnected player may reconnect and continue while the match is active, but must complete their turn before the server's turn deadline. The deadline is the configured turn duration plus a 5-second allowance. If the server detects that the player whose turn it is has disconnected before that deadline, it extends the existing deadline by 20 seconds, at most once per player per match. Reconnecting does not restart the timer or grant a new extension. A player who fails to complete their turn before the applicable deadline is treated as having left the match for its result. In a two-player match, the opponent who remains wins; with more players, the remaining players continue under the game rules.

11.2. No automatic annulment for a disconnection. An interruption of a player's internet connection, whether intentional or accidental, is not by itself a reason to annul a match or reverse its in-game consequences.

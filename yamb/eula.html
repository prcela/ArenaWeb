11. Connection Interruptions, Time Limits and Match Review

11.1. Reconnecting and completing a turn. In turn-based multiplayer matches, a disconnected player may reconnect and continue while the match is active, but must complete their turn before the server's turn deadline. The deadline is the configured turn duration plus a 5-second allowance. If the server detects that the player whose turn it is has disconnected before that deadline, it extends the existing deadline by 20 seconds, at most once per player per match. Reconnecting does not restart the turn timer or grant a new extension. A player who fails to complete their turn before the applicable deadline is treated as having left the match for the purposes of its result. In a two-player match, the opponent who remains wins; in matches with more players, the remaining players continue under the applicable game rules.

11.2. A disconnection does not automatically void a match. An interruption of a player's internet connection, whether intentional or accidental, is not by itself a reason to annul a match or reverse its in-game consequences. This also applies where the player had not yet made a move. A claim that the player's internet connection was otherwise working normally does not, by itself, establish a fault in Yamb's system or server.

11.3. Assessment by an administrator or moderator. An administrator or moderator may annul a match under this review procedure only where a reasonable assessment of the available evidence establishes a Yamb system or server error that affected the fair conduct or outcome of the match. The assessment considers available server and system logs, match records and other relevant information, including information supplied by the player. The same criteria apply consistently to all players, and the decision must be based on the circumstances of the match.

11.4. Inconclusive information. Technical records may not conclusively show whether a connection was interrupted intentionally or accidentally. An inability to determine the cause does not, by itself, establish either deliberate misconduct by the player or a system or server error. If the review does not establish an error of the kind described in section 11.3, the recorded match result remains in effect under the game rules, subject to the player's mandatory legal rights. Applying the ordinary timeout result does not require a finding of intentional misconduct.

Kartaš - Privacy Policy

Rika Omega Rika provides Kartaš as a free app (the Service). Kartaš is a card-playing game with online multiplayer and chat. It does not include real-money gambling or simulated gambling.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use Kartaš. We use this information to provide and improve the Service and do not use or share it except as described in this policy.

Information Collection and Use

We may ask you to choose a nickname for use in online multiplayer, the global leaderboard, and chat. You do not need to use your real name. Your nickname is visible to other users when you use these features. We retain and use your nickname to provide these features.

Chat and User Content

Kartaš includes chat so that players can communicate with each other. When you choose to send a message, the app processes its content to deliver and display it to other participants in that chat. Your messages may contain personal information if you choose to include it.

Messages you send are visible to other participants in the chat, who may copy or share them. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information, such as your address, phone number, passwords, or payment details, or personal information about other people.

Log Data

If an error occurs while you use the app, we collect diagnostic information through third-party products to help identify and resolve the problem. This Log Data may include your nickname, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other usage statistics.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Please review this page periodically for changes. Updates will be posted on this page and take effect when posted.
